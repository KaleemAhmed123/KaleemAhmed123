### What I do

I build products end to end, with most of my depth in backend and distributed systems and a specialisation in production AI. I take an idea from a POC through the messy middle — where requirements change, trade-offs become real, and the system has to become more than a demo — and own the engineering underneath: service boundaries, data flows, infrastructure, reliability, failure modes.

### What I enjoy

What happens *after* something works. What should happen when the same event arrives twice. When two requests touch the same wallet in the same moment. When one service is down and the rest still has to keep moving. Most of it I learned by getting it wrong first — a race condition or an assumption that only held on the happy path teaches me more than a clean implementation ever does.

### AI, honestly

AI is a force multiplier in the way I build; I keep full engineering ownership of what ships — every output reviewed, challenged, overridden where judgment says so. On RAG and agentic systems the hard part is rarely the model — it's the surrounding system: grounding, context, when retrieval is actually needed, and the guardrails for when the safest answer is not to guess. The durable advantages are still human: focus, discipline, adaptive learning, product taste. &nbsp;→&nbsp; **[More](https://kaleemahmed.in/thoughts/how-i-use-ai)**
